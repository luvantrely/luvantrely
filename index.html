Privacy Policy.

By Ved Prakash | Executive Protection

1. Data Sovereignty

At Luvantrely, your data is treated as elite assets. We collect your identity (Name, DOB, Gender) and bio solely to curate your experience within the circle. We do not sell your personal archives to any third-party marketing entities.

2. Geolocation & Privacy

To maintain intimacy, we access your location only to find matches within your chosen discovery radius. Your precise coordinates are never visible to other members; only an approximate distance is displayed.

3. Media & Content

Any media uploaded to Luvantrely is processed via secure cloud servers. We reserve the right to audit content to maintain the "Elite" standard of our community, ensuring a safe environment free from harassment.

4. Cookies & Tracking

We use "Elite-Tokens" (Cookies) to remember your session. This ensures you stay logged into the circle without re-authenticating every time you open the app.

5. Account Termination

You have the "Right to be Forgotten." Upon deleting your account, all your personal records, matches, and logs will be permanently purged from our active databases within 30 days.
